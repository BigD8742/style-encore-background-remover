Erase removes leftover background. Restore paints back parts of the product the AI cut off (the original photo shows faintly to guide you). Paint with one finger; pinch with two fingers to zoom and move.

Drag the product with one finger to move it. Pinch with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller. It snaps to the center when it gets close. Square and portrait are positioned separately.
