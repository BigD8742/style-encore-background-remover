Keep

"Keep" sets every photo at once. You can still tap a picture below to add or remove it. Square is 1:1, portrait is 4:5 (Instagram's tall post).

Everything runs on this device. Your photos are never uploaded. The first run downloads the AI model (about 90 MB, best on Wi-Fi) and may take a minute; after that it is much faster. Keep this screen open while it works.

Touch up
Brush

Erase removes leftover background. Restore paints back parts of the product the AI cut off (the original photo shows faintly to guide you). Paint with one finger; pinch with two fingers to zoom and move.
